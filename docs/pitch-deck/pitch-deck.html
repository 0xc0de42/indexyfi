IndexFi gives you one ERC-20 that represents a whole basket. Three properties: it’s backed by real collateral, it can be created or redeemed for USDT at NAV, and it trades on PancakeSwap. Investors hold a transferable claim on a basket instead of an isolated fund position.

How it works

From basket to tradable token

1

Choose

Pick assets and fixed starting weights that sum to 100%. Constituents never change.

2

Fund

Put in at least 100 USDT of collateral, plus a separate liquidity amount of at least 1 USDT.

3

Acquire

The vault buys every asset on PancakeSwap. Shares are issued on the value actually acquired, at 1 USDT NAV.

4

Graduate

OpenFour moves the token to a PancakeSwap V2 pool priced at NAV. The creator gets the LP tokens, unlocked.

Creator keeps the backed shares not placed in the pool. No manager, no rebalancing: weights drift with prices.

IndexFi · BNB Bangkok 2026

A creator picks the assets and weights, which never change. They fund collateral and initial liquidity separately. Step 3 is the heart of it: the collateral USDT is split by weight and swapped into each asset on PancakeSwap, with slippage protection on every leg. The vault then counts what it actually received, not what the quote promised, values those holdings with Atlas on-chain prices, and mints one share per 1 USDT of value, rounded down. Swap costs therefore reduce the share count instead of diluting anyone, so NAV starts at 1 USDT. Finally OpenFour graduates the token to a PancakeSwap V2 pool seeded at NAV, and the creator gets unlocked LP tokens. The target is for steps 3 and 4 to run in one transaction.

The primary market is the vault: deposits buy the basket at current weights and mint new backed shares; redemptions sell your pro-rata slice of every holding back to USDT. Redemption doesn’t need an oracle at all. The secondary market is an ordinary PancakeSwap V2 pool where existing shares change hands at the market price. The two prices can differ, and that gap is what arbitrage closes.

Simple numbers. The creator puts 100 USDT into collateral and 10 USDT into liquidity. That backs 100 shares at a NAV of 1 USDT: 90 go to the creator and 10 are paired with the 10 USDT in the pool, so the pool opens at NAV. When someone deposits 100 USDT, 10 bps goes to the creator, the remainder buys the basket, and shares are minted against what was actually acquired, so the depositor bears their own slippage.

Arbitrage

Arbitrage pulls price toward NAV

Premium: price above NAV

  1. Deposit USDT, get new shares at NAV
  2. Sell them on PancakeSwap
  3. Selling pushes the price down

Discount: price below NAV

  1. Buy shares on PancakeSwap
  2. Redeem them for pro-rata USDT
  3. Buying pushes the price up

Dashed line: NAV. Solid line: pool price. Worth doing only after the 10 bps fee, swap costs, slippage and gas; convergence isn’t guaranteed.

IndexFi · BNB Bangkok 2026

This is the distinctive part. Because issuance and redemption are permissionless, anyone can arbitrage. If the token trades at a premium, deposit at NAV and sell into the pool. At a discount, buy from the pool and redeem. Either trade moves the market price toward NAV. It only works when the spread beats fees, slippage and gas, and thin liquidity limits it. In the demo we show this on a controlled local pool, clearly labeled.

Fees

10 bps in.

Fixed fees on primary deposits and redemptions, paid to the creator in USDT. Creators can also earn swap fees on their own LP position.

No management fee

No performance fee

No transfer tax

The business model is deliberately boring. 10 basis points on every primary deposit and every redemption, paid to the creator automatically in USDT. No management fee, no performance fee, no tax on transfers, and no fee knobs to tune. On top of that the creator earns returns on their own shares and swap fees from the LP tokens they hold.

Architecture

What IndexFi is built on

Web app

Next.js, wagmi/viem and RainbowKit. Create, deposit, redeem and trade from one place.

Price server

Python service streaming 66 live Atlas feeds for the asset browser and charts.

IndexFi contracts

Factory, multi-asset basket vault and preset validator, with Atlas valuation and PancakeSwap route adapters and fixed 10 bps fees.

OpenFour

Core, Registry, token and V2 migration library, reused as-is.

Atlas

On-chain reference NAV for issuance and the opening pool price.

PancakeSwap V2

Executes the basket swaps and hosts the secondary pool.

IndexFi · BNB Bangkok 2026

Reuse first. We use OpenFour’s official Core, Registry, token and migration library, and only write what a basket needs: a custom multi-asset vault, a preset validator and a factory. Atlas provides the on-chain reference price used for issuance; PancakeSwap executes the swaps and hosts the pool. The Python server only displays live Atlas prices: it holds no keys and is never used as an on-chain price source.
