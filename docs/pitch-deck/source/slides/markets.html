The primary market is the vault: deposits buy the basket at current weights and mint new backed shares; redemptions sell your pro-rata slice of every holding back to USDT. Redemption doesn’t need an oracle at all. The secondary market is an ordinary PancakeSwap V2 pool where existing shares change hands at the market price. The two prices can differ, and that gap is what arbitrage closes.
