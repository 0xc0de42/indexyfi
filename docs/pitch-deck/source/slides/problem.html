The problem

Diversified on-chain exposure is still a pile of separate positions

Many swaps

A hand-built basket means one swap, one approval and one position to track per asset.

Locked shares

Fund-style vaults often issue non-transferable positions: no secondary market, no composability.

No anchor

A basket token that can’t be redeemed for its backing has nothing pulling its price back to value.

IndexFi · BNB Bangkok 2026

Today, getting diversified exposure on-chain means buying every asset yourself, or depositing into a vault that hands you a position you can’t move or trade. And a basket token without redemption has no mechanism tying its market price to what it holds.
