Fixed fees on primary deposits and redemptions, paid to the creator in USDT. Creators can also earn swap fees on their own LP position.

The business model is deliberately boring. 10 basis points on every primary deposit and every redemption, paid to the creator automatically in USDT. No management fee, no performance fee, no tax on transfers, and no fee knobs to tune. On top of that the creator earns returns on their own shares and swap fees from the LP tokens they hold.
