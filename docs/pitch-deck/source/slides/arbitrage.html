Arbitrage

Arbitrage pulls price toward NAV

Premium: price above NAV

  1. Deposit USDT, get new shares at NAV
  2. Sell them on PancakeSwap
  3. Selling pushes the price down

Discount: price below NAV

  1. Buy shares on PancakeSwap
  2. Redeem them for pro-rata USDT
  3. Buying pushes the price up

Dashed line: NAV. Solid line: pool price. Worth doing only after the 10 bps fee, swap costs, slippage and gas; convergence isn’t guaranteed.

This is the distinctive part. Because issuance and redemption are permissionless, anyone can arbitrage. If the token trades at a premium, deposit at NAV and sell into the pool. At a discount, buy from the pool and redeem. Either trade moves the market price toward NAV. It only works when the spread beats fees, slippage and gas, and thin liquidity limits it.
